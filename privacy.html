1. Data Collection

Twistfall does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, device identifiers, usage analytics, or location data. All gameplay progress, high scores, stars, and settings (such as theme, language, and audio preferences) are stored exclusively locally on your device.

Twistfall contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking technologies.
