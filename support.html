Frequently Asked Questions

How do I rotate the board?

Drop a falling block so that it locks inside one of the highlighted gold spin columns on the left or right of the core. The entire 13×13 field will rotate 90° around the central core, and any blocks not anchored to the core or an anchor stone will settle under gravity.

How do I earn item charges and Axiom cards?

Complete campaign levels to earn up to 3 stars per level and unlock new Axiom cards. Clearing objective targets during play also awards item charges for Break Anchor, Reshape Next, and Expand Board.

How do I switch languages or visual themes?

Tap the gear icon in the top-right corner of the screen to open Settings, where you can switch between English and Simplified Chinese as well as four visual themes (Obsidian, Paper, Candy, and Orchid).
